## 📈 MARKETING & SALES CHANNELS

### 1. 1-Click OAuth Integration (Meta & Google)
* **The Goal**: Replace manual Pixel/CAPI entry with a seamless "Login with Facebook" and "Login with Google" flow that automatically provisions tracking and catalogs.
* **The Delay Reason**: Requires massive administrative overhead. We must register as an official Meta Business App and undergo Google Cloud External Security Assessments to get scopes for `ads_management` and `business_management`. Average approval time: 4-8 weeks.
* **Prerequisites**: Verified Company Registration, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and a live production app to record screencasts for the reviewers.
