## 🏗️ CORE PRODUCT PILLARS (`strat-positioning-basics`)

### 1. Built for Selling (Conversion-First)
We are not a "design tool." Every pixel in Easy D2C is engineered to close the sale. We remove dangerous design decisions from the seller.
- **The Hook:** "You want sales. Not settings."
- **Mechanism:** Structured conversion architecture and opinionated layouts.

### 2. Visitor Maximization (Ad-Efficiency)
Traffic costs money; losing it is criminal. We guide buyers forward instinctively.
- **The Hook:** "Ads are expensive. Losing customers is worse."
- **Mechanism:** Native AOV boosters, structured CTA hierarchy, and variant-aware pricing.

### 3. Stupidly Simple (Tech-Agnostic)
We value ambition over tech skill. If you can upload a Reel, you can launch a store.
- **The Hook:** "No tech degree required. Go live tonight."
- **Mechanism:** No plugin dependencies. Pre-installed systems. Minimal config surface.

### 4. Ridiculous Value (Price-to-Performance)
₹199 to launch. You spend more on coffee or a single day of ads.
- **The Hook:** "Cheap in price. Expensive in value."
- **Mechanism:** Transparent pricing ladder (₹199 → ₹249 → ₹499). No hidden add-ons.

### 5. Culturally Aligned (India-First)
Built for the COD reality, return anxiety, and cultural nuances of Indian e-commerce.
- **The Hook:** "Built for COD. Not California."
- **Mechanism:** Native COD logic, RTO-aware design, and vernacular-friendly messaging.
