# Bharat PDP – Ultimate Conversion Package (North Star)

## Purpose
This document defines the **north star conversion system** for Bharat D2C Platform. It captures the *ideal, opinionated, Shopify-beating* PDP experience that removes seller anxiety and maximises conversion, AOV, trust, and long-term scalability.

This is **not a theme** and **not a list of apps**.
It is a **pre-installed D2C growth stack**, embedded natively into the platform.

---

## Core Philosophy

**Seller promise:**
> “This setup is sales-focused by default. You only worry about ads.”

**Platform stance:**
- No app-store chaos
- No widget clutter
- No trial-and-error anxiety
- Opinionated, proven defaults

Everything ships **pre-installed**, but **invisible unless needed**.

---

## PDP = Conversion System (Not Sections)

The PDP is designed as **conversion clusters**, each owning a buyer decision.

### Canonical Buyer Flow
1. Recognition & Legitimacy
2. Value Clarity
3. Decision (Conversion)
4. Anxiety Reduction
5. Rational Justification

---

## PDP-Basic Conversion Clusters (v1)

### 1️⃣ Hero Cluster – Recognition
**Purpose:** Immediate context + legitimacy

Contains:
- Image gallery
- Product title
- Rating & review count

---

### 2️⃣ Highlights Cluster – Value Communication
**Purpose:** Answer *“Why should I care?”* before price is shown

Contains:
- 3–5 benefit-driven bullets
- Icon/tick-based highlights
- Problem → outcome framing

> Critical for dropshipping, utility, and impulse products

---

### 3️⃣ Conversion Cluster – Decision Engine (MOST IMPORTANT)
**Purpose:** Close the sale

This is a **single visual unit**, never fragmented.

Contains:
- Effective price (variant-aware)
- MRP + savings
- Discounts & offers
- Primary trust signals (COD, returns, shipping)
- Primary CTA

Also hosts:
- Urgency signals
- Free shipping progress
- Quantity breaks

---

### 4️⃣ Proof Cluster – Social Validation
**Purpose:** Reduce anxiety

Contains:
- Reviews & testimonials
- Verified buyer indicators
- Optional micro-proof nudges (rate-limited)

---

### 5️⃣ Content Cluster – Rational Justification
**Purpose:** Convince logical buyers

Contains:
- Description
- How it works
- FAQs
- Policies (contextual)

Implemented via accordions

## Pre-Installed Growth Capabilities (Native)

### Trust System
- COD availability & eligibility
- Returns & refund clarity
- Shipping & delivery estimates
- Secure payment indicators

Trust is a **layer**, not a section.

---

### Urgency & Scarcity Engine
- Stock-based urgency
- Time-based urgency (dispatch / COD cutoff)
- Behavioral urgency (future)

Never fake. Never spammy.

---

### Pricing, Discounts & Offers
- Flat / % discounts
- Buy X Get Y
- Free shipping thresholds
- Auto-apply best coupon

Removes coupon anxiety.

---

### AOV Boosters
- Quantity breaks
- Bundles & combos
- Related / complementary products
- Inline savings communication

---

### Popups & Nudges (Controlled)
Only canonical popups exist:
- Exit intent
- First-time visitor offer
- COD reassurance
- Free shipping nudge

Rule-driven, design-system consistent.

---

### Reviews System (Judge.me-class, Native)
- Star ratings
- Text + photo reviews
- Verified buyer badges
- SEO-friendly schema

Single system reused everywhere.

---

### SEO & Visibility (Native)
- Auto meta tags
- Product, Review, FAQ schema
- Image SEO
- Share-friendly OG cards

SEO works by default.
