Return to Origin is the defining margin problem for Indian D2C sellers. COD-driven RTO costs the industry an estimated ₹15,000+ crore annually in forward and reverse logistics, tied-up inventory, and lost working capital. Every seller who has built their own mitigation — blanket pincode blocks, static blacklists, OTP walls — has done so by sacrificing reach to reduce risk.
Easy D2C's RTO Intelligence Engine takes a different position: minimise RTO without minimising reach. Not by blocking bad orders — by converting risky orders into safe ones, and building the intelligence to know the difference.
Layer 1: The Scoring Engine — Calculation Logic
1A. Base Scoring Matrix
Every COD order receives a composite risk score from 0 to 100. Kill passes override all calculation and assign maximum score instantly.
1B. Extended Scoring Signals
Additional signals layered on the base matrix. These require no external API — they are derived from order data and platform behavior.
1C. Category Risk Multipliers
Each product category carries a baseline RTO risk multiplier, seeded from industry benchmarks and updated continuously from platform delivery data. Applied as a final multiplier on the composite score.
1D. Session Behavior Signals — The Unique Layer
This is the signal layer no standalone RTO tool can replicate. Easy D2C's Adaptive Engine is already tracking session behavior for conversion optimization. At the moment of COD checkout, the RTO Engine reads those same signals as a risk modifier. Zero additional infrastructure. Structurally defensible.
1E. The Living Pincode Intelligence Database
The pincode risk database is not a static download. It is a continuously self-updating dataset that learns from every delivery outcome across every store on the platform.
At launch: seeded from IndiaPost API, publicly available logistics-difficult region data, and industry RTO benchmarks
From day one of live orders: every delivery outcome — delivered, bounced, refused, RTO — is logged against pincode, category, order value band, and time of order
Emerging spike detection: if a pincode's 14-day rolling RTO rate spikes above a threshold, it is automatically elevated across the entire platform — not just for the store that experienced it
Recovery logic: pincodes that show 90 consecutive days of clean delivery are automatically downgraded from their elevated risk tier
Category specificity: a pincode may be low-risk for Electronics and high-risk for Fashion — the database knows this and scores accordingly
The network effect is the moat: a seller with 50 orders a month cannot build this intelligence alone. A platform with 2,000 stores and 100,000 orders a month can. Every store's delivery outcomes make every other store's scoring engine smarter.

Layer 3: Automated Actions — What the Engine Does
Sellers set their risk tolerance once. The engine executes forever. Every action is auditable, overridable, and explained in plain language.
3A. The Action Matrix — Score to Intervention
3B. The Intervention Flows
Flow 1 — WhatsApp COD Confirmation (40–54 band)
Triggered automatically within 2 minutes of order placement.
Three outcomes: Reply 1 → order proceeds, intent confirmed. Reply 2 → voluntary cancellation, logistics cost saved. No reply in 4 hours → escalated to next intervention tier.
Flow 2 — Partial Prepaid Gate (55–69 band)
The checkout page dynamically presents partial prepaid as the primary option for this specific order. The amount is calculated dynamically: minimum of (logistics cost estimate for that pincode) or (8% of order value), whichever is lower. This is a psychology play as much as a risk play — it creates skin in the game. Fraudsters and casual clickers don't pay anything. Real buyers usually will.
Flow 3 — Prepaid Incentive Engine (cross-tier, configurable)
At any risk score above 30, the engine can be configured to offer a prepaid incentive alongside the COD option rather than replacing it. Works well for sellers with strong brand trust where hard gating would damage perception.
Flow 4 — Auto-Cancellation with Graceful Exit (85–100 band)
Order is cancelled before entering the fulfillment queue. Two messages go out simultaneously:
To buyer: 'We're unable to process COD orders to your location right now. You can place a prepaid order using this link, or we'll notify you when COD becomes available in your area.'
To seller dashboard: '[1 order auto-cancelled. Phone number matched 2 previous bounces on your store. Estimated logistics cost protected: ₹160. Override and Fulfill]'
3C. The Dynamic COD Restriction Engine
This replaces the blunt instrument of blanket COD blocks by geography. Instead, the engine maintains a dynamic COD availability matrix per store, updated continuously from platform delivery data.
Sellers set their COD risk tolerance once: Conservative / Balanced / Aggressive
The engine automatically restricts COD for pincode × category combinations that exceed the seller's chosen threshold
COD is automatically restored when a pincode recovers (90-day clean window) — risk tiers are not permanent punishments
Never applies a blanket block — always pincode-and-category specific, preserving maximum legitimate reach
Every restriction and restoration surfaced to the seller with a plain-language reason
A seller in the Conservative setting gets intelligent COD management without ever thinking about pincodes again. A seller in the Aggressive setting gets flagged orders but maximum reach. Both make an informed choice once.
3D. The Prepaid Conversion Tracker — Closing the Loop
Every automated intervention that results in a prepaid conversion is quantified in rupees and surfaced back to the seller monthly.
This metric — COD-to-prepaid conversions driven by RTO Shield — becomes one of the headline numbers in the monthly Intelligence report. It directly answers the retention question every seller asks each month: is this platform making me more money than I'm paying for it?
