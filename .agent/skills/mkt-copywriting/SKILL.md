# Conversion Copywriting

## Core Principles
- **Clarity Over Cleverness**: The reader should understand exactly what you offer within 5 seconds.
- **Benefits Over Features**: Always answer "What's in it for me?"
- **Specificity Over Vagueness**: Use numbers, concrete outcomes, and real examples.
- **Customer Language**: Mirror how your customers actually talk.

## Frameworks
- **AIDA**: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action.
- **PAS**: Problem, Agitation, Solution.
- **The 4 U's**: Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific, Useful.

## Page Structure
1. **Above the Fold**: Headline (Benefit), Subheadline (How it works), Primary CTA.
2. **Social Proof**: Testimonials, logos, stats.
3. **Features/Benefits**: Grouped logically with scannable headers.
4. **Objection Handling**: FAQ or trust-building sections.
5. **Final CTA**: High-contrast, action-oriented button.
