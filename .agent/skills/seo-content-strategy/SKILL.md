# Content Quality & E-E-A-T Analysis

## E-E-A-T Framework (Google 2025 Standards)

### Experience
- Original research, case studies, before/after results.
- Personal anecdotes, unique data.

### Expertise
- Author credentials, bio, and technical depth.

### Authoritativeness
- External citations, brand mentions, and industry recognition.

### Trustworthiness
- Contact info, testimonials, secure site (HTTPS), and transparent corrections.

## AI Citation Readiness (GEO Signals)
Optimize for AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews):
- **Structured Highlights**: Clear, fact-dense blocks.
- **Answer-First**: Provide direct answers in the first 40-60 words of a section.
- **Data Densification**: Use tables and lists for comparative data.
- **Attribution**: Clearly cite all sources and claims.

## Quality Benchmarks
- Flesch Reading Ease: 60-70 target for accessibility.
- Heading Hierarchy: Proper H1 -> H2 -> H3 flow.
- Scannability: Bullet points and short paragraphs.
