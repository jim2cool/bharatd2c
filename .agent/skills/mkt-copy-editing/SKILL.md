# Copy Editing & Polishing

## The Seven Sweeps Framework
1. **Clarity Sweep**: Remove jargon and complex sentences. Can a 5th grader understand this?
2. **Voice & Tone Sweep**: Ensure consistent personality and alignment with brand guidelines.
3. **"So What?" Sweep**: For every claim, ask "So what?". If it doesn't lead to a benefit, cut it.
4. **"Prove It" Sweep**: Add data, testimonials, or specific examples to every major claim.
5. **Specificity Sweep**: Replace vague words (e.g., "fast", "easy") with concrete ones (e.g., "under 2 seconds", "1-click").
6. **Emotion Sweep**: Heighten the desire or contrast between the problem and solution.
7. **Zero Risk Sweep**: Find points of friction and add risk-reversal (guarantees, free trials).

## Quick Quality Checks
- Check for the "Peeping Tom" problem (using "your" and "you" more than "we" and "I").
- Remove weasel words (e.g., "perhaps", "maybe", "try").
- Ensure one clear CTA per section.
