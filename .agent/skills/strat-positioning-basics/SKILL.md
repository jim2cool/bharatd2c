# Brand Positioning Basics

## The Core 5 Questions
1. **WHO is this for?**: Be ultra-specific about the target customer (Vertical + Persona).
2. **WHAT problem do you solve?**: What's the "job" it's hired to do?
3. **HOW do you solve it?**: What's your unique mechanism or approach?
4. **WHY is this better?**: How are you different from the status quo or competitors?
5. **SO WHAT?**: What's the high-level outcome or transformation?

## Positioning Statement Template
> For **[target audience]** who are frustrated with **[current problem]**, **[our product]** is a **[category description]** that **[uniquely solves it]** unlike **[main competitor]** because **[unique differentiator]**.

## Competitive Mapping
Don't just list features. Map based on:
- **Value**: High-touch vs. Automated.
- **Speed**: Instant vs. Deep.
- **Audience**: Professional vs. Beginner.
Find the "White Space" where you can be #1.
