# AI Search / GEO Optimization (2026)

## Critical Insight: Brand Mentions > Backlinks
Brand mentions correlate 3× more strongly with AI visibility than backlinks. Focus on:
- YouTube mentions
- Reddit discussions
- Wikipedia/Wikidata presence

## GEO Analysis Criteria

### 1. Citability Score
Optimal passage length: **134-167 words** for AI citation.
- Self-contained answer blocks.
- Direct answer in the first 50 words.
- Specific statistics and facts included.

### 2. AI Crawler Accessibility
Check `robots.txt` for:
- **GPTBot** (OpenAI)
- **ClaudeBot** (Anthropic)
- **PerplexityBot** (Perplexity)

### 3. llms.txt Standard
Implement a `/llms.txt` file at the root to provide structured content guidance for AI crawlers.

## Output Structure
- **GEO Readiness Score (0-100)**
- **AI Crawler Audit**
- **Citability Optimization Plan**
- **Brand Signal Map**
