# Marketing Idea Generator

## Idea Categories
- **Quick Wins**: Low effort, immediate impact (e.g., fixing a CTA, updating a headline).
- **Growth Loops**: Ideas that feed into themselves (e.g., referral programs, user-generated content).
- **Authority Building**: High effort, long-term gain (e.g., whitepapers, original research).
- **Product-Led Growth**: Using the product to market itself (e.g., "Powered by" links, free tools).

## The PIE Framework (Prioritization)
- **Probability**: How likely is this to succeed? (1-10)
- **Impact**: How much difference will it make? (1-10)
- **Ease**: How easy is it to implement? (1-10)
Score = (P + I + E) / 3.

## Ideation Prompts
- "What if we did the opposite of the market leader?"
- "How can we make the first 5 minutes of the product magical?"
- "What free tool could we build to solve a prospect's #1 frustration?"
