# Marketing Psychology & Triggers

## Foundational Mental Models
- **Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)**: Users don't buy products; they "hire" them to do a job.
- **Pareto Principle (80/20)**: 80% of results come from 20% of your copy/features.
- **Loss Aversion**: The pain of losing is $2\times$ stronger than the joy of gaining.

## Key Psychological Triggers
- **Reciprocity**: Give value (free trial, guide) before asking for anything.
- **Social Proof**: Reviews, case studies, and "join 5,000 others."
- **Scarcity/Urgency**: Countdown timers, limited seats, or exclusive offers.
- **Authority**: Highlighting certifications, media mentions, or expert endorsements.
- **Anchoring**: Showing a "normal" price first to make the "sale" price look better.

## Pricing Psychology
- **Charm Pricing**: Pricing at $99 instead of $100.
- **The Decoy Effect**: Adding a middle option that makes the expensive one look like a bargain.
- **The IKEA Effect**: People value things more if they helped create them (onboarding wizards).
